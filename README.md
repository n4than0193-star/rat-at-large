Rat- At-Large is a chaotic little stealth-action game I whipped up for Jame Gam originally, and now this! Rat-At-Large is only my third game ever, and it puts you in the paws of a tiny rat trapped in a house full of giants. The humans want you gone, but you are not going down without a fight. Armed with nothing but quick reflexes and a pocket full of trusty marbles, you have to outmanoeuvre and defeat a different human foe across two action-packed levels.

The gameplay is all about dodging heavy footsteps, hiding in the shadows, and slinging marbles when the timing is right. Since it was made during an event, I honestly ran out of time right at the end. It is definitely a bit rough around the edges and missing some final polish, but the core game is fully complete and playable from start to finish.

Play it now at https://n4th4ni3l.itch.io/rat-at-large !
